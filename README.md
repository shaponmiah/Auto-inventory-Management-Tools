# 📦 Smart Inventory Pro

> Browser-based inventory & profit management system built for UK multi-platform resellers.
> Fully local — no server, no monthly fees, your data never leaves your device.

---

## ✨ Features

📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- Real-time KPIs: stock levels, values, gross profit, revenue
- Click cards to jump directly to relevant pages
- 6-month sales chart · VAT summary · PDF profit reports

📦 Stock Management
- Product catalogue: name, SKU, category, cost, price, margin, photo, barcode
- Low Stock / Out-of-Stock alerts with restock suggestions
- Bulk Stock In Queue · Printable barcode labels · CSV import/export
- Full backup & restore support

💰 Sales Tracking
- Platforms: eBay · Vinted · Etsy · Facebook Marketplace · Depop · Custom
- Single Sale + Multi-Item Basket — record multiple items in one click
- Returns & Lost in Transit tracking · Top Products · Sales by Platform
- Activity Log with Undo — fix mistakes instantly

🎨 Premium UI
- Claymorphic design · Animated space background
- Colour-coded hover buttons: ✅ Green | ❌ Red | 💜 Purple
- Smooth tab & sidebar animations · Fully responsive

🇬🇧 UK Ready
- Gross profit = Sale Price − Cost Price
- VAT estimation · Export sales log for accountant · Self-employed friendly

---

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Download the `smart-inventory-uk.html` file
2. Open it in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari)
3. Start adding → Categories → Products → Record Sales
4. Export Backup regularly to save your data

> All data is stored locally in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.
